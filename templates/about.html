About QuantEngine

QuantEngine is a full-stack investment scenario calculator that takes the guesswork out of financial planning.

Instead of getting a single "you'll have X in 30 years" number, QuantEngine generates three scenarios: base, optimistic, and pessimistic. This shows you the realistic range of outcomes for your investments.

Because the future isn't a single number. It's a probability distribution.

What It Does

Three-Scenario Modelling — Base, optimistic, and pessimistic projections for every investment scenario. See possibilities, not just predictions.

Risk Appetite Selection — Choose your tolerance (Low/Medium/High) with visual risk selector cards.

Scenario History — Save and name your scenarios (Retirement Fund, House Deposit) to compare strategies over time.

User Authentication — Secure registration and login. Your scenarios are private and persistent.

Responsive Design — Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
